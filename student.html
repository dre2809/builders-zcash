ZIP-TBD — Ironwood Network Upgrade

NU6 — Dev Fund Restructure
Activated November 2025. Changed the block reward split to 80% miners, 8% ZCG grants, 12% community lockbox reserve awaiting decentralized disbursement.
Passed
ZIP-317 — Proportional Transfer Fee
Standardised transaction fees: base fee + per-action fee in zatoshis. Protects the mempool from spam and keeps fees predictable for developers.
Passed
ZIP-316 — Unified Addresses
Merged transparent, Sapling, and Orchard receivers into a single address format. Wallets automatically use the most private available pool.
